### What this prototype can show

Inspect a question with explicit requested facts, run the frozen lookup pipeline,
and follow each numbered citation to its source cell. The three cards compare
**extraction-only, binary sufficiency and graded sufficiency** on identical
retained evidence. These are the frozen pilot baselines, not the later local-model
research comparison. **The local model is off.**

### Pilot scope

The six prepared examples reuse existing controlled pilot tables. Partial and
empty-scope examples concern those supplied tables, not everything a company
reported. The conflict is a **controlled test case** containing the pilot's
existing artificial alternative value; it is not a genuine report disagreement.
No values or policies were tuned to make the demonstration pass.

The prior pilot has **18 independent source facts and 36 derived cases; no
statistical or performance claims are made from it**. Its labels are
**provisional, single-annotator**. The questions share contexts; independence is
not established. No evaluation/development questions or labels are loaded here.

### Source and format limits

The pipeline supports literal metric labels with explicit year columns and
unambiguous numeric values. Multi-level or duplicate headers, ambiguous segments,
unit changes and blank/dash values may remain unverified. **Header repair,
automatic decomposition and unit conversion are unsupported.** A parsing error
or unsupported format is not evidence that a real-world fact is absent.

Source-cell links identify the submitted table snapshot. They do not authenticate
an issuer filing. Green cells support the returned graded answer. Amber cells
are baseline references and are not a guarantee of correctness. Graded answers
withhold all values and answer citations when the unchanged policy detects a
conflict, even if another requested fact could be answered.

### Retrieval and claims

Retrieval and packing are bounded and may omit support or conflicting evidence.
A full result concerns the evidence retained for that run, not corpus-wide
completeness. Unresolved report overlap remains accepted under Option C;
**hash/company-name deduplication cannot establish disjointness**. There are no
claims of independence between questions, generalization to other documents,
statistical superiority or acceptable deployment risk.

The three cards are descriptive behavior comparisons, not performance scores.
A human second annotator must independently review all 30 future evaluation
questions before any paper results. No corpus is locked and no future evaluation
data is selected by this app.
