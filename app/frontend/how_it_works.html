1. RequestSupply a question, explicit requested facts and one table snapshot. No automatic question decomposition.
  2. RetrievalFind lexical matches in a temporary local index. Pack whole evidence units within the fixed input budget.
  3. RequirementsCarry the same explicit metric and year requirements into every comparison policy. They were supplied before retrieval.
  4. VerifierCheck candidate values against retained canonical rows. Unsupported formats remain unverified; conflicts follow the frozen rule.
  5. DecisionReturn full, a supported subset, or abstain. A detected conflict withholds the whole graded answer.
  6. CitationsFilter graded citations to supporting evidence and map them to original submitted cells. Baseline references may be unsafe.
