# Source-only partial authoring audit

**Provisional first pass. No performance or statistical claims.** The
[40-group audit](partial-availability-and-controlled-conflict-plan.md) produced
**23 distinct proposed partial drafts in 15 groups**. The numerical draft count
exceeds 15, but no draft has the accountable author's confirmation or independent
human verification. **The audit does not certify availability for the nine
evaluation and six development partials. Stop at feasibility.** No amendment
was drafted, and no quota or eligibility rule changed.

The roster was frozen using seed 1729 and the updated exclusion graph, with
the previous 20 partial-audit groups excluded and no replacements. The first
pass screened 232 instantiated pairs, with up to two per template per group.
Proposed positives required one directly supported fact and one plausible fact
absent from the complete declared excerpt scope after frozen keyword searches
and a full table and prose check. Unreadable or ambiguous entries and unavailable
resolving references were not counted as absence. Retained drafts were checked
for reused requested facts, with at most two retained per group. This cannot
establish that separately identified groups come from distinct reports.

The proposed group yield is **15/40 (37.5%)** unweighted and **58.12%** weighted
to the fresh frame. Counting the unresolved groups as positive instead gives
**29/40 (72.5%)** and **83.60%**, respectively, conditional on all first-pass
positives holding. These are sensitivity endpoints for the bounded procedure,
not confidence intervals or corpus-wide availability estimates. Until human
review, the confirmed count is zero; that does not imply zero genuine partials.
The draft count does not certify joint allocation under the group cap and the
other strata. No draft is eligible for automatic promotion into the study.

rejections** for the user's spot check remain in ignored private files. No

decoded, zero system outputs were inspected, and zero evaluation or development
