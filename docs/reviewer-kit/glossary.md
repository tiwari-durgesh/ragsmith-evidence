# Plain-language glossary

These are reading aids, not permission to infer an unstated answer.

| Term | Meaning for this task |
|---|---|
| Metric / fact | A named quantity, such as Revenue or Cash. Match the supplied row label and scope. |
| Revenue | Money earned from sales during a stated period; not automatically profit or cash. |
| Cash | Money reported as cash for the stated date; not the same as revenue. |
| Profit / net income | A reported earnings measure after the stated expenses. Do not calculate it here. |
| Assets / liabilities | Things the entity owns/controls, and obligations it owes. They are different metrics. |
| Property and equipment, net | A reported carrying value after stated deductions such as depreciation; not necessarily market value. |
| Noncontrolling interest | The portion attributable to owners other than the parent company; do not substitute total equity. |
| Year / period | The column's reporting year or date. A year mentioned in text may not label the adjacent value. |
| Unit | The scale/currency, e.g. USD millions. Printed 12 means 12 in that scale; preserve it without conversion. |
| Parentheses | Often a negative amount, e.g. (3). Preserve the raw text rather than guessing context. |
| Dash / blank | May mean not applicable, not reported or another convention. It does not establish zero. |
| Scope / segment | Which entity, division, version or class the value concerns. Different scopes need not conflict. |
| Requirement | One explicitly requested metric and year, judged separately before a question-level decision. |
| Citation | A source-cell address supporting a value, not simply a cell that mentions the same year. |
| Contradiction | Incompatible eligible values with the same metric, year, unit and scope, with no source resolution. |
| Abstention | A policy withholds an answer. The reviewer judges source evidence independently of that policy. |
| Provisional | A label awaiting independent review/adjudication; not established research gold. |
| Calibration | Choosing a confidence threshold using authorized development data. Not part of reviewer practice. |

You may write “unit unclear” or “row meaning unclear” in notes. Financial
arithmetic, external lookup and header repair are outside this task.
